About Rheumderma Clinic

Rheumderma Clinic is a combination speciality clinic to address the needs of patients with rheumatic conditions and skin disorders. Situated at Prabhadevi, behind Shree Siddhivinayak Temple. Providing compassionate, evidence-based care for autoimmune, skin, and mental health conditions.
